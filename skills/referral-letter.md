name: referral-letter
description: Write specialist referral letters for veterinary patients
---

Write a professional veterinary specialist referral letter based on the case information provided.

The referral letter should include:

**Header**
- Referring veterinarian's name, clinic name, address, phone, and email
- Date
- Recipient specialist's name, specialty, and clinic/hospital name

**Salutation**
- Professional greeting addressing the specialist by name

**Patient Signalment**
- Patient name, species, breed, age, sex, reproductive status
- Owner name and contact information

**Reason for Referral**
- Clear statement of why the patient is being referred
- Specific questions or concerns you would like the specialist to address

**History and Clinical Summary**
- Presenting complaint and duration
- Relevant medical and surgical history
- Current medications and doses
- Diet and lifestyle factors
- Key physical examination findings
- Results of diagnostics performed (bloodwork, urinalysis, imaging, biopsies, cultures)
- Response to any treatments attempted

**Differential Diagnoses / Working Diagnosis**
- Your current assessment and clinical reasoning
- Specific areas of uncertainty prompting the referral

**Referral Request**
- Services requested (consultation, advanced diagnostics, procedure, surgery, second opinion)
- Any time-sensitive concerns (urgent vs. routine)

**Closing**
- Offer to provide additional records or discuss the case
- Thank the specialist
- Professional sign-off with credentials

Write in a collegial, professional tone appropriate for veterinary specialist-to-specialist communication. Be thorough and organized so the specialist has all relevant information before the appointment. Use standard veterinary terminology and abbreviations where appropriate.
