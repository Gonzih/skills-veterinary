---
name: discharge-instructions
description: Write clear patient discharge instruction sheets for veterinary patients
---

Create clear, client-friendly discharge instructions for a veterinary patient based on the visit details provided.

The discharge instructions should include:

**Patient Information**
- Patient name, species, breed, age
- Date of visit / procedure
- Attending veterinarian

**Reason for Visit / Diagnosis**
- Plain-language explanation of the condition or procedure
- Brief description of what was done during the visit

**Medications**
For each medication prescribed:
- Drug name (generic and brand name)
- What it is for (in plain language)
- Dose and how to give it (pill, liquid, injection, topical)
- Frequency and exact schedule
- Duration of treatment
- Important warnings (give with food, avoid sun, complete full course, etc.)
- What to do if a dose is missed

**Home Care Instructions**
- Activity restrictions (rest, no running/jumping, leash walks only, etc.)
- Dietary instructions (special diet, fasting requirements, water restrictions)
- Wound or bandage care (if applicable)
- How to monitor the patient at home

**What to Watch For**
- Signs of improvement expected
- Warning signs that require immediate veterinary attention
- Signs that warrant a follow-up call to the clinic

**Follow-Up Care**
- Scheduled recheck appointment date/time (if applicable)
- When to call the clinic
- Clinic phone number and after-hours emergency contact

Write in clear, simple language that a non-medical pet owner can easily understand. Avoid jargon. Use numbered lists or bullet points for readability. Include a friendly, reassuring tone. End with the clinic contact information and a reminder to call with any questions.
